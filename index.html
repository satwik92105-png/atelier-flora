The Devotion Behind Every Botanical Piece

01

Wildflower Foraging

Daisies, forget-me-nots, and ferns gathered ethically from organic local gardens and sunlit meadows at peak blossom.

02

Slow Botanical Pressing

Pressed inside heavy wooden presses for 21 days between absorbent blotting sheets to lock in radiant natural petal pigments.

03

Artisan Hand-Pouring

Eco-resin poured layer-by-layer over 72 hours, eliminating bubbles and sealing blooms in glass-like protective clarity.

04

Needlework & Ceramics

Embroidered stitch-by-stitch with silk threads and hand-pinched in stoneware clay before high-temperature kiln firing.
